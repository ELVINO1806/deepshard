How mining works

Mining is time-based. Your miner earns SHARD while time passes; one claim a day settles it. No hardware, no proof-of-work, nothing running on your machine.

1 · Mint free

Hold an Agent ID, connect your wallet, mint. Max 2 miners per wallet, 3,333 total — locked in code.

2 · Claim daily

First claim starts your clock. After that, claim once a day (20-hour cooldown). Each claim also digs +4 m into the shared depth — +8 m on your class day.

3 · Keep the streak

You have a 48-hour grace window. Streaks multiply SHARD: day 3 ×1.1, day 7 ×1.25, day 14 ×1.5, day 30 ×2.0.

4 · Level up

Lifetime SHARD raises your level, from Lv 1 to Lv 10. Levels never decrease. SHARD itself never burns and is never taken away.

What SHARD is — and is not. SHARD is an in-game points record kept inside the contract. It is not a token: it cannot be transferred, has no price, and is never promised to become one. Holders receive SHARD points, levels, and Founder Miner status. Nothing else is promised, by design.

Questions

Who can mint?

Any wallet that holds an Agent ID. The contract checks the official SPACE ID Agent ID registry on-chain: registry balance above zero, a non-empty handle, and the handle resolving back to your wallet. There is no social requirement — no follow, like, or repost is needed to mint.

What does it cost?

The mint itself is free (price 0). You pay only the BNB Smart Chain network gas fee for your transaction, which goes to the chain — the project receives nothing from minters.

What do holders receive?

SHARD points, levels, and Founder Miner status for Season 1. SHARD is an in-game points record, not a token: it is non-transferable, has no price, and is never promised to become one. No airdrop or monetary reward is promised.

What is the Agent ID Hackathon?

DeepShard is an independent entry in the Agent ID Hackathon (Binance Wallet × BNB Chain), where it competes for the builders' Innovation Award pool of 29,000 USDC. That pool is a prize for builders, decided by the organizers — it is not a prize for minters, and no part of it is promised to holders.

When does Season 1 end?

Season 1, The First Descent, is open now. Minting runs until the 3,333 supply is gone, and daily claiming continues after that. The shared goal is the 10,000 m depth target — reached together, whenever the crew gets there.

Can I lose my SHARD or my streak?

SHARD never decreases and is never burned. A streak resets only if you miss more than the 48-hour grace window between claims — and even then, your SHARD and level stay exactly where they were.
